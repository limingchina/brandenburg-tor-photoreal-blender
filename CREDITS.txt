Brandenburg Tor Photoreal V5

V5 POSE AND FACIAL REVISION
The outer horses are positioned at +/-2.02 m and use a verified evaluated 15.5-degree outward yaw after comparison with the user's initial frontal quadriga photograph. The inner pair remain nearly frontal at +/-2 degrees. Bridle, breast-collar, rein, and trace curve endpoints follow the revised outer poses.

Each horse has two recessed dark oxidized eyes seated inside small bronze eyelid rims. The ears are supplemented with tapered, beveled outer shells and nearly flush darker inner-ear inserts; all facial-detail objects are parented to their respective horse roots.

MATERIAL SOURCES
The v4 albedo source textures were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool and converted locally into a 2K PBR atlas (albedo, roughness, tangent-space normal, and metallic). The generated sources were color- and weathering-calibrated against the public-domain Brandenburg Gate photographs listed below. No perspective photo projection is embedded in the model.

V4/V5 CHANGES
- Photo-informed 2K four-channel PBR atlas for sandstone and patinated bronze.
- Metric wrapped texture coordinates to avoid stretching on long facade elements.
- Layered bronze normal response: atlas relief plus shader-scale cast-metal micro-pitting.
- Imported quaternion-root issue corrected; outer horses are truly yawed 15.5 degrees outward and inner horses 2 degrees.
- Eight recessed eyes, eight eyelid rims, and refined two-layer ear geometry.
- New frontal facade and three-quarter quadriga reference renders.
