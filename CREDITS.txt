Brandenburg Tor Photoreal V4

MATERIAL SOURCES
The v4 albedo source textures were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool and converted locally into a 2K PBR atlas (albedo, roughness, tangent-space normal, and metallic). The generated sources were color- and weathering-calibrated against the public-domain Brandenburg Gate photographs listed below. No perspective photo projection is embedded in the model.

V4 CHANGES
- Photo-informed 2K four-channel PBR atlas for sandstone and patinated bronze.
- Metric wrapped texture coordinates to avoid stretching on long facade elements.
- Layered bronze normal response: atlas relief plus shader-scale cast-metal micro-pitting.
- Outer horses widened and yawed 11.7 degrees outward; inner pair yawed 2.3 degrees.
- New frontal facade and three-quarter quadriga reference renders.
